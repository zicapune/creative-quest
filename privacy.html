Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · ZICA Pune, Kothrud

The ZICA Creative Quest is a free creative quiz run by ZICA Pune (Kothrud). This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Your quiz: the questions you saw, your answers and your score.
  • Your details: name, email address, mobile number, and the course you're interested in (optional).
  • If you choose "Continue with Google": only your name and email address from your Google account, used to fill in the form and confirm your email is real. We don't get your password, contacts, files or anything else from Google.
  • Your choices: whether you agreed to hear from us about courses and events, and when.
  • Basic technical data: device type (phone, tablet or computer) and the link you came from.

Why we use it

  • To run the quiz and show your score.
  • To create, deliver and verify your certificate.
  • To give you access to our free introductory lessons.
  • To contact you about ZICA Pune courses, workshops and events on WhatsApp or email only if you ticked that box.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored securely with Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India) and copied to a private Google Sheet used only by the ZICA Pune team. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Your certificate

Each certificate has a public verification page (from its QR code) that shows the name on the certificate, the date and the certificate ID — nothing else.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your details, or to stop messaging you, at any time. Contact the ZICA Pune team at our Kothrud centre, or reply to any message we send you.

Children

If you are under 18, please take the quest with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.
